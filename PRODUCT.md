# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two sides of cross-border trade, both evaluating GÜVEN BİRLİK as an intermediary:

- **Foreign buyers** (CIS, Europe, Middle East, Asia, Africa) looking for a reliable partner or supplier in Türkiye.
- **Turkish producers** looking for a route to export markets.

Both arrive with a concrete need (a product, a volume, a destination, a timeline) and decide whether to start a conversation. The site is read in Turkish and English.

## Product Purpose

The site presents GÜVEN BİRLİK İÇ VE DIŞ TİCARET A.Ş., an Istanbul trading company, and turns a visitor's need into an inquiry. Success means a qualified inquiry by email: product, quantity, direction and timing.

## Positioning

An Istanbul-based intermediary that handles **any goods on request**. It is not a single-category supplier: it sources and connects to fit the client's task. Istanbul's position between Europe and Asia is the factual anchor. "GÜVEN" means "trust", and the company treats trust as its stated principle.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare intermediaries before committing. They need to quickly understand what the company does (import, export, trade connections), where it operates from, and how to start. Inquiries go by email. The form opens the visitor's mail app with a prepared message, and nothing is sent to or stored on a server.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: plain HTML/CSS/JS in `dist/`, deployed to Vercel (`*.vercel.app` temporary domain) from GitHub `malik-alaska2/guven-birlik-site`.
- TR (default) and EN via `app.js` dictionaries; the markup itself is Turkish. Russian was removed at the owner's request.
- WebGL globe (three.js r160, vendored) with illustrative trade directions from Istanbul: Berlin, Tashkent, Dubai, Shanghai, Casablanca.
- Live, computed data only: distances, local times, sun position, shared business hours.
- Contact: email only (guvenbirlikicvedisticaret@gmail.com) and office address. No phone or messenger numbers.
- Some visitors' networks block `*.vercel.app`, so the site cannot be checked from the owner's own connection.

## Compliance

The company does not work with countries or parties under international sanctions, or with high-risk jurisdictions (owner's instruction). Directions, examples and copy must never feature such destinations. The site states this in the Geography footnote.

## Product Principles

1. Trust over hype: state only what is true, and keep the next step clear.
2. Two-sided clarity: buyers and producers should each see their path in seconds.
3. Istanbul as the bridge: geography is the real differentiator, so show it, don't decorate it.
4. One action: every section leads to a single inquiry by email.
